  - optimize — one round. LLM profiles, picks the hot function, proposes ONE patch, measures, accepts-or-reverts. ~5–10k tokens.
  Good for "show me what's possible here" with a known cost.
  - run — full agent loop (up to budget.max_patches_proposed iterations, default 10), then full pytest regression check, then PR.md
  assembly. ~30–100k tokens depending on how far it gets. Good for "do the real work."

  Two flags worth knowing on run:
  - --skip-regression — skips the full pytest suite at the end (the suite is huge and slow on static-frame); useful while iterating
